情境 The situation

一名初级开发在项目中自主使用 AI 辅助编码。最近一段时间，他交付功能的速度明显变快，也能尝试接手之前很难独立完成的需求。但团队 review 的时候，大家发现他对部分代码的底层逻辑并不熟悉。

A junior developer voluntarily uses AI to write code. Recently, he has been delivering features much faster and can take on requirements he previously struggled to complete alone. However, during team reviews, the team notices that he does not understand the underlying logic of some of the code.

情境 The situation

一段时间之后，这名初级开发继续依靠 AI 产出代码，团队不少人也跟着高频使用 AI。一次线上故障追溯时发现，问题就出在一段 AI 生成的代码；更麻烦的是，当初写代码的人，也没法清晰解释它的底层逻辑。整个团队在排查时非常被动，独立排障效率大打折扣。有同事提议，限制初级开发者使用 AI。

The junior developer keeps relying on AI to produce code, and many teammates now use AI frequently too. During a production incident review, the root cause turns out to be AI-generated code. Worse, its original author cannot clearly explain the underlying logic. The team struggles badly during troubleshooting, and independent diagnosis slows dramatically. A colleague proposes restricting junior developers’ access to AI.

问题 Question

As the manager, how would you respond to the proposal? What factors would you prioritise?

03 / 03
情境 The situation

那次线上故障之后，团队最终没有一刀切禁止 AI，而是落地了差异化使用规则：常规开发允许使用 AI，但故障复盘、复杂问题攻坚必须独立思考。一段时间之后，你观察到这名初级开发：常规开发任务完成得又快又好，但总是回避高难度、需要独立深度推演的任务，很少主动挑战超出当前能力的工作。作为他的管理者，你打算怎么支持他持续成长？

After the production incident, the team decides not to ban AI entirely. Instead, it adopts differentiated rules: AI is permitted for routine development, while incident reviews and complex problem-solving require independent thinking. Later, you observe the junior developer completing routine tasks efficiently, but consistently avoiding difficult assignments that demand independent, in-depth reasoning. He rarely volunteers for work beyond his current comfort zone. As his manager, how will you support his continued growth?
